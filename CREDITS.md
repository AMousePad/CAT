## User-supplied PNGs

The cardboard box and cat avatar were supplied by the project owner. Their source filenames and modifications are recorded in [credits.json](src/ui/icons/custom/credits.json). No additional license was supplied. Copies were cropped to their non-transparent pixels and resized for UI controls, preserving their colors and transparency.
